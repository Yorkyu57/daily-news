Umaru Yakubu Kirawa, a local journalist, confirmed that residents reported hundreds of abductions and numerous deaths. He highlighted the community's desperation, noting that while some managed to break their fast, others could not before the violence erupted. Kirawa emphasized that this is the first attack on Ngoshe since the government resettled residents who had previously been displaced due to insecurity. Borno state has long been the epicenter of Nigeria's Islamist insurgency, plagued by Boko Haram and Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP) fighters.
