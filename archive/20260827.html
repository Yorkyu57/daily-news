  • FDA批准首款可连续监测酮体水平和血糖的可穿戴设备
    美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）已批准Libre Duo 10天连续双葡萄糖酮体监测系统用于2岁及以上糖尿病患者。该设备标志着重要的里程碑，它是美国首个能够连续监测酮体水平的可穿戴设备，也是全球首个在单一设备中同时追踪酮体和血糖（葡萄糖）的设备。 美国食品药品监督管理局器械与放射健康中心（CDRH）主任米歇尔·塔弗（Michelle Tarver）医学博士、哲学博士将此次授权描述为对患者安全的突破。塔弗表示：“知道酮体水平正在上升，并全天候实时掌握这一信息，可能成为早期干预与危及生命的紧急情况之间的关键区别。” 美国疾病控制与预防中心估计，4010万美国人患有糖尿病。对于大约210万患有1型糖尿病的美国人来说，仅监测血糖是不够的。当身体使用脂肪而非葡萄糖作为能量来源时，会产生过量的酮体，这可能导致糖尿病酮症酸中毒（DKA），这是一种发展迅速且可能危及生命的并发症。此前，酮体监测需要单独的测试，这些测试仅提供单点测量值，无法指示水平是上升还是下降。Libre Duo 10天系统通过每分钟、昼夜不停地测量皮下液体中的酮体和葡萄糖水平，解决了这一局限性。读数通过无线方式传输到兼容的智能手机上，显示当前水平和趋势。如果酮体水平达到令人担忧的阈值，该系统会自动发出警报，从而有可能在DKA成为紧急情况之前早期识别酮体升高。然而，酮体数据必须始终结合血糖读数和症状进行解读。 美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）的这项决定是在对该器械申办者提交的临床和性能数据进行全面审查之后作出的。该临床项目包括六项研究，共纳入600多名两岁及以上的受试者，以评估该器械在不同酮体和血糖水平下的准确性。结果证实，该器械在其10天的佩戴期内能够准确追踪具有临床意义的酮体水平差异，包括在糖尿病酮症酸中毒（DKA）发生前识别出升高的酮体水平。 此项授权与FDA的“家庭作为医疗中心”倡议相一致，该倡议旨在推广创新的、以患者为中心的、能够无缝融入日常家庭生活的器械。在最终批准之前，FDA已授予该器械“突破性器械”认定，该认定旨在加速那些为危及生命或不可逆致残性疾病提供更有效治疗或诊断的技术的开发和审查。 该器械的市场营销授权通过De Novo上市前审查途径授予雅培糖尿病护理公司，该途径适用于新型的低至中度风险器械。同时，FDA制定了特殊控制措施，规定了标签和性能测试的要求。当这些特殊控制措施与一般控制措施相结合并得到满足时，可为该器械的安全性和有效性提供合理保证。FDA是美国卫生与公众服务部下属的机构，通过确保医疗器械、药品、疫苗及其他生物制品的安全性、有效性和安全性来保护公众健康。
    [Original] FDA Authorizes First Wearable Device That Continuously Monitors Both Ketone Levels and Blood Sugar
    The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has authorized the Libre Duo 10 Day Continuous Dual Glucose Ketone Monitoring System for individuals aged two years and older living with diabetes. This device marks a significant milestone as the first wearable in the United States to continuously monitor ketone levels and the first globally to track both ketones and blood sugar (glucose) simultaneously in a single unit. Michelle Tarver, M.D., Ph.D., Director of the FDA’s Center for Devices and Radiological Health, described the authorization as a breakthrough for patient safety. “Knowing that ketone levels are rising, and having that information in real time, around the clock, can be the difference between early intervention and a life-threatening emergency,” Tarver stated. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention estimates that 40.1 million Americans live with diabetes. For the approximately 2.1 million Americans with type 1 diabetes, monitoring blood sugar alone is insufficient. Excessive ketone production, which occurs when the body uses fat instead of glucose for energy, can lead to diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), a rapidly developing and potentially life-threatening complication. Previously, ketone monitoring required separate tests that provided only single-point measurements, failing to indicate whether levels were rising or falling. The Libre Duo 10 Day addresses this limitation by measuring ketone and glucose levels in subcutaneous fluid every minute, day and night. Readings are transmitted wirelessly to a compatible smartphone, displaying current levels and trends. The system issues automatic alerts if ketone levels reach concerning thresholds, potentially enabling early recognition of rising ketones before DKA becomes an emergency. However, ketone data must always be interpreted alongside glucose readings and symptoms. The FDA’s decision follows a comprehensive review of clinical and performance data submitted by the device sponsor. The clinical program comprised six studies enrolling more than 600 participants aged two and older to evaluate accuracy across various ketone and glucose levels. Results confirmed the device accurately tracked clinically meaningful differences in ketone levels over its 10-day wear period, including identifying elevated levels prior to DKA onset. This authorization aligns with the FDA’s Home as a Health Care Hub Initiative, which promotes innovative, patient-centered devices that integrate seamlessly into daily home life. Prior to final approval, the FDA granted the device Breakthrough Device designation, a status intended to expedite the development and review of technologies offering more effective treatment or diagnosis for life-threatening or irreversibly debilitating conditions. Marketing authorization was granted to Abbott Diabetes Care through the De Novo premarket review pathway, a regulatory route for low- to moderate-risk devices of a new type. Concurrently, the FDA established special controls defining requirements for labeling and performance testing. When these special controls are met in combination with general controls, they provide a reasonable assurance of the device’s safety and effectiveness. The FDA, an agency within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, protects public health by assuring the safety, effectiveness, and security of medical devices, drugs, vaccines, and other biological products.

  • 使用 Sentence Transformers 训练和微调多向量嵌入模型
    Sentence Transformers 是一个用于训练和使用嵌入模型及重排序模型的 Python 库，现已发布 6.0 版本，引入了用于 ColBERT 风格后期交互检索的 MultiVectorEncoder 模型类型。此次更新包含一套全面的训练方法，旨在帮助用户微调在特定数据集上性能优于通用检索器的多向量模型。该方法支持微调现有模型和从头训练新模型，所有组件均可通过 `sentence-transformers[train]` 包访问。 多向量模型与稠密嵌入模型的不同之处在于，它保留每个词元（token）的一个小向量，而不是将文本压缩为单个向量。它们利用 MaxSim 算子，其中每个查询词元匹配其最佳匹配的文档词元，从而保留了单向量模型平均化后丢失的细粒度信号。虽然这种方法带来了更强的检索性能，但会导致索引体积增大。微调至关重要，因为通用模型通常将文档截断为 180 到 512 个词元，这一限制在长文档任务（如平均段落长度为 941 个词元的医学检索）中可能导致高达 0.24 的 NDCG@10 损失。 训练过程涉及多个组件：模型、数据集、损失函数、训练参数、评估器和训练器类。用户可以通过调整长度配置以处理更长的文档，并添加标点符号跳过列表（punctuation skiplists）将索引大小减少 9.6%，从而微调现有检查点，例如 `lightonai/mLateOn-unsupervised`。或者，用户可以基于 `answerdotai/ModernBERT-base` 等基础 Transformer 构建模型，该模型会自动附加一个随机初始化的词元级投影。实验表明，在强大的骨干网络上使用新的投影，仅使用 25,000 个训练对即可达到与现有检查点相差 0.03 以内的性能。 对MIRIA数据集中25,000个医学问题-段落对进行六个起始点测试的一项关键发现是，无监督检查点在适应新领域方面显著优于其有监督对应模型。这些预监督模型保留了晚期交互结构，且未经过通用调优，在领域适应过程中超越了完全训练完成的检查点，而完全训练完成的模型往往会出现性能回退。因此，建议从预监督检查点开始，而非从完全训练完成的模型继续。 在实际演示中，作者在单块RTX 3090 GPU上花费14.5小时对`multi-vector-encoder/mLateOn-medical`模型进行了微调。该模型在医学检索评估中超越了所有通用检索模型——包括稠密、稀疏、词汇和多向量变体。该库支持来自Hugging Face Hub或本地格式（如CSV、JSON、Parquet、Arrow或SQL）的数据，使得在消费级硬件上高效开发特定领域模型成为可能。
    [Original] Training and Finetuning Multi-Vector Embedding Models with Sentence Transformers
    Sentence Transformers, a Python library for training and utilizing embedding and reranker models, has released version 6.0, introducing the MultiVectorEncoder model type for ColBERT-style late interaction retrieval. This update includes a comprehensive training approach designed to help users finetune multi-vector models that outperform general-purpose retrievers on specific datasets. The method supports both finetuning existing models and training new ones from scratch, with all components accessible via the `sentence-transformers[train]` package. Multi-vector models differ from dense embedding models by retaining one small vector per token rather than compressing text into a single vector. They utilize the MaxSim operator, where each query token matches its best-matching document token, preserving fine-grained signals that single-vector models average away. While this approach yields stronger retrieval performance, it results in larger indices. Finetuning is critical because general-purpose models often truncate documents at 180 to 512 tokens, a limitation that can cost up to 0.24 NDCG@10 on long-document tasks, such as medical retrieval where passages average 941 tokens. The training process involves several components: the model, datasets, loss functions, training arguments, evaluators, and the trainer class. Users can finetune existing checkpoints, such as `lightonai/mLateOn-unsupervised`, by adjusting length configurations to handle longer documents and adding punctuation skiplists to reduce index size by 9.6%. Alternatively, users can build models from base transformers like `answerdotai/ModernBERT-base`, which automatically appends a randomly initialized token-level projection. Experiments show that a fresh projection on a strong backbone can reach within 0.03 of existing checkpoints using only 25,000 training pairs. A key finding from testing six starting points on 25,000 medical question-passage pairs from MIRIAD is that unsupervised checkpoints adapt to new domains significantly better than their supervised counterparts. These pre-supervised models, which retain late-interaction structure without general-purpose tuning, overtake finished checkpoints during domain adaptation, whereas finished models often regress. Consequently, starting with a pre-supervised checkpoint is recommended over continuing from a fully finished model. In a practical demonstration, the author finetuned the `multi-vector-encoder/mLateOn-medical` model in 14.5 hours on a single RTX 3090 GPU. This model outperformed all general-purpose retrieval models—including dense, sparse, lexical, and multi-vector variants—on a medical retrieval evaluation. The library supports data from the Hugging Face Hub or local formats such as CSV, JSON, Parquet, Arrow, or SQL, enabling efficient domain-specific model development on consumer hardware.
