    英伟达推出Cosmos-H-Dreams，这是一款面向手术机器人的实时动作条件生成式模拟器，旨在解决在物理机器人平台上训练和评估视觉-语言-动作策略所面临的挑战。物理系统成本高昂、复现速度慢，且存在损坏器械或生物组织的风险，而传统模拟器难以模拟涉及可变形组织、缝合线、烟雾和遮挡的复杂手术场景。 Cosmos-H-Dreams基于早期的Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator构建，后者是一个基于英伟达Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B构建的动作条件世界基础模型，并在Open-H-Embodiment数据集上进行了后训练。该模型能够根据初始场景和机器人动作生成未来手术视频，从而实现比物理评估更快的评估速度。新系统将这些能力提炼为一个因果式、少步数的学生模型，并通过英伟达的加速流式推理库FlashDreams提供服务。在单个英伟达RTX PRO 6000 GPU上运行时，它提供了一个可由人类或学习策略以闭环方式控制的交互式环境。 蒸馏过程包含三个阶段。首先，在JHU dVRK桌面混合数据集上对双向教师模型进行微调，该数据集包含成功演示以及针头掉落、投掷失误等失败案例，以确保模拟器能够复现不良操作导致的后果。教师模型的时间跨度从12帧逐步增加至72帧。其次，从教师模型初始化因果学生模型，并训练其模仿预计算的去噪轨迹，学习使用因果注意力机制和流式键值缓存进行运算。第三，自强制蒸馏解决了在干净上下文训练与不完美输出部署之间的不匹配问题：学生模型利用自身生成的上下文进行前向推理，由冻结的教师模型提供监督，引导输出向真实视频趋近。最终模型每个潜变量帧仅需两步去噪即可完成。 FlashDreams通过流式KV缓存、CUDA图捕获和模型编译优化推理性能，在单块RTX PRO 6000上将处理速度从约每秒10帧提升至约每秒160帧。人机交互接口包括通过WebRTC发送键盘命令的浏览器客户端，以及通过WebXR将追踪控制器运动映射为机器人操作的Meta Quest客户端。该模型还能以闭环方式连接已学习的手术策略。NVIDIA已与CMR Surgical及Cambridge Consultants合作，将Cosmos-H-Dreams集成至Versius手术机器人控制器。 该系统被设计为可扩展的，为自定义数据集提供了完整的教师微调和自强制蒸馏方案。未来工作包括评估工具尖端精度、夹爪循环保真度、空闲稳定性和长期漂移，并探索在延迟感知远程手术、交互式手术预演和术中决策支持中的应用。Cosmos-H-Dreams是一个研发平台，而非诊断系统或术中成像的替代品。
    [Original] NVIDIA Cosmos-H-Dreams: Bringing Real-Time Generative Simulation to Surgical Robotics
    NVIDIA has introduced Cosmos-H-Dreams, a real-time, action-conditioned generative simulator for surgical robotics, designed to address the challenges of training and evaluating vision-language-action policies on physical robotic platforms. Physical systems are expensive, slow to reproduce, and risk damaging instruments or biological tissue, while conventional simulators struggle to model complex surgical scenes involving deformable tissue, sutures, smoke, and occlusions. Cosmos-H-Dreams builds on the earlier Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator, an action-conditioned world foundation model built on NVIDIA Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B and post-trained on the Open-H-Embodiment dataset. That model generated future surgical video from an initial scene and robot actions, enabling faster-than-physical evaluation. The new system distills those capabilities into a causal, few-step student model served through FlashDreams, NVIDIA’s accelerated streaming-inference library. Running on a single NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU, it provides an interactive environment controllable by a human or learned policy in a closed loop. The distillation process involves three stages. First, a bidirectional teacher model is fine-tuned on the JHU dVRK tabletop mixture, including both successful demonstrations and failure episodes such as needle drops and missed throws, to ensure the simulator reproduces the consequences of poor actions. The teacher’s temporal horizon is progressively increased from 12 to 72 frames. Second, a causal student is initialized from the teacher and trained to imitate precomputed denoising trajectories, learning to operate with causal attention and a streaming key/value cache. Third, self-forcing distillation addresses the mismatch between training on clean context and deploying on imperfect outputs: the student rolls forward using its own generated context, with supervision from the frozen teacher guiding outputs toward realistic video. The resulting model supports as few as two denoising steps per latent frame. FlashDreams optimizes inference through streaming KV cache, CUDA Graph capturing, and model compilation, boosting performance from roughly ten frames per second to approximately 160 frames per second on a single RTX PRO 6000. Human-machine interfaces include a browser client sending keyboard commands over WebRTC and a Meta Quest client mapping tracked controller motion into robot actions via WebXR. The model can also connect to learned surgical policies in a closed loop. NVIDIA has collaborated with CMR Surgical and Cambridge Consultants to integrate Cosmos-H-Dreams with the Versius surgeon controller. The system is designed to be extensible, with a complete recipe for teacher fine-tuning and self-forcing distillation provided for custom datasets. Future work includes evaluating tool-tip accuracy, gripper-cycle fidelity, idle stability, and long-horizon drift, as well as exploring applications in latency-aware telesurgery, interactive surgical rehearsal, and intraoperative decision support. Cosmos-H-Dreams is a research and development platform, not a diagnostic system or replacement for intraoperative imaging.
