This trend extends beyond FuturLab; Skyhook Games' Lawn Mowing Simulator lets players run a lawn care business using "real-world licensed lawnmowers." David Harper, managing director of Skyhook Games, described the appeal as the satisfaction of bringing order to an untidy scene. An Oxford University study involving over 8,600 players found that 72% experienced a mood uplift while playing. FuturLab plans to continue expanding their portfolio to become the "market leader in cathartic games," maintaining their unique ethos of providing pleasant, low-octane entertainment.
