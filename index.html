    Pharma marketers are actively planning for 2027, facing a new budgeting imperative: building share of answer (SOA). Clinicians increasingly use large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT and Claude for both personal and clinical queries. In response, pharma marketers are shifting strategies toward answer engine optimization. Onikoro noted that while teams have made strides in adapting websites for AI crawlers, 2027 will see a significant allocation of budgets to paid AI campaigns. However, AI platforms are touting substantial impressions-based inventory at high costs, creating risks when investment decisions lack audience context. Many marketers currently lack specific audience data for AI platforms, particularly the continuous insights needed to adapt to changing HCP behavior. “While every platform can likely pitch impressive reach, that reach only matters if your customers are there,” Onikoro stated, warning that overinvesting in platforms where physicians are absent leads to poor ROI. To mitigate this, marketers are turning to affinity data, which PharmaForceIQ maps dynamically to HCP behavior across consumer LLMs and clinical-specific AI platforms. Some clinical platforms require HCPs to register using their national provider identifier, offering additional signals on tool usage. Beyond location, marketers seek to understand what HCPs are seeking and why. Onikoro explained that agent plug-ins allow brands to provide authoritative information that models reference when responding to queries. This exchange also generates signals regarding topics and keywords, helping marketers refine investment and content. Although many marketers approach SOA with trepidation due to the rapidly changing landscape, the right data can clarify decisions. Onikoro highlighted that AI offers the potential for one-on-one interactions with HCPs, informing answers to specific questions similarly to a brand marketer or sales rep. “This will be an evolution, of course, but the next phase is very exciting, potentially giving marketers a new way to connect and communicate with their customers,” he said. By leveraging dynamic, continuously updated views of audience behavior, pharma marketers aim to deliver more efficient campaigns and secure a prominent position in the AI-driven clinical information ecosystem.
