  • 欧洲委员会四十余年来引领动物福利发展
    四十多年来，欧洲委员会及欧洲药品与卫生保健质量局（EDQM）一直引领着全球动物福利工作。五项公约确立了首批规范动物运输、养殖、屠宰及科学用途的国际法律文书。值此《用于实验和其他科学目的的脊椎动物保护欧洲公约》（公约 ETS 第 123 号）通过四十周年之际，该组织回顾了其取得的重大成就及面临的未来挑战。 1986 年 3 月 18 日，欧洲委员会邀请成员国签署公约 ETS 第 123 号，从而启动了将科学领域动物使用的"3R 原则”（替代、减少和优化）付诸实践的努力。EDQM 下属的欧洲药典委员会（EPC）开始审查《欧洲药典》（Ph. Eur.）中的动物试验，该药典是 39 个成员国的具有法律约束力的标准。其目标是修订文本以引入符合 3R 原则的方法，证明无动物替代方案既支持伦理方法，又能促进精确且具成本效益的检测。这一工作需要进行广泛的验证和磋商，从而深刻塑造了欧洲乃至全球的药品监管体系。 1991 年，与欧盟共同建立的生物标准化计划（BSP）加速了疫苗等生物制品的进展，促使符合 3R 原则的方法被纳入《欧洲药典》的专论中。2010 年，《2010/63/EU 号指令》的通过标志着又一重大里程碑。该指令紧密基于公约 ETS 第 123 号，通过引入程序严重程度分类，进一步强化了 3R 原则。其第 13 条规定，在有可用替代方案时必须使用无动物替代方法，从而隐含地承认了欧洲委员会的开创性作用。 《欧洲药典》中的重大删减减少了动物使用。2013 年，兽用疫苗的目标动物批次安全性试验被取消。2019 年，异常毒性试验（ATT）从 49 个专论中删除。最近，家兔热原试验（RPT）被完全取消，标志着向体外热原性控制的转变。此外，涉及豚鼠和猫的组胺及降压物质通则章节也被删除。自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起，重组因子 C（rFC）将作为鲎变形细胞溶解物（LAL）方法的替代方案，纳入通则 2.6.14，从而减轻对受保护鲎的压力。 这些进展为终结对基于动物方法的依赖铺平了道路，在确保患者安全的同时，开启了一个创新与可持续的时代。“移除”这一概念已获得动力，有效地为传统的三个"R"增添了第四个"R"。尽管进展缓慢但坚定，进一步的国际合作仍然至关重要。近期举办的“热原检测 2.0：伦理、演进与环保”研讨会，重申了向无动物检测全球转型的承诺。如今已进入第五个十年的《欧洲委员会第 123 号欧洲条约》（ETS No. 123），继续履行其在科学领域提升动物福祉的承诺。
    [Original] Council of Europe leading the way in animal welfare for over 40 years
    For over four decades, the Council of Europe and the European Directorate for the Quality of Medicines & HealthCare (EDQM) have led global efforts in animal welfare. Five conventions established the first international legal instruments governing animal transport, farming, slaughter, and scientific use. Marking the fortieth anniversary of the European Convention for the Protection of Vertebrate Animals used for Experimental and Other Scientific Purposes (Convention ETS No. 123), the organization reflects on significant achievements and future challenges. On 18 March 1986, the Council of Europe invited member states to sign Convention ETS No. 123, initiating efforts to transpose the "3R principles"—replacing, reducing, and refining animal use in science—into practice. The EDQM's European Pharmacopoeia Commission (EPC) began reviewing animal tests in the European Pharmacopoeia (Ph. Eur.), the legally binding standards for 39 member states. The goal was to amend texts to introduce 3R-compliant methods, demonstrating that animal-free alternatives support ethical approaches while fostering precise, cost-effective testing. This undertaking required extensive validation and consultation, profoundly shaping medicinal product regulation across Europe and worldwide. In 1991, the Biological Standardisation Programme (BSP), established jointly with the European Union, accelerated progress for biological products like vaccines, leading to the inclusion of 3R-compliant methods in Ph. Eur. monographs. A major milestone followed in 2010 with the adoption of Directive 2010/63/EU. Closely based on Convention ETS No. 123, the Directive reinforced the 3R principles by introducing severity classifications for procedures. Article 13 mandates the use of animal-free alternatives when available, implicitly recognizing the Council of Europe's pioneering role. Significant deletions from the Ph. Eur. have reduced animal use. In 2013, the target animal batch safety test for veterinary vaccines was removed. In 2019, the abnormal toxicity test (ATT) was deleted from 49 monographs. More recently, the rabbit pyrogen test (RPT) was completely removed, marking a shift toward in vitro pyrogenicity control. Additionally, general chapters on Histamine and Depressor substances, which utilized guinea pigs and cats, were deleted. As of 1 January 2027, recombinant factor C (rFC) will be included in general chapter 2.6.14 as an alternative to limulus amoebocyte lysate (LAL) methods, reducing pressure on protected horseshoe crabs. These advances pave the way for an end to reliance on animal-based methods, ushering in an era of innovation and sustainability while maintaining patient safety. The concept of "removal" has gained momentum, effectively adding a fourth "R" to the traditional three. While progress has been slow but sure, further international collaboration remains essential. The recent symposium, "Pyrogen testing 2.0: Ethical, Evolving and Eco‑friendly," renewed commitment to the global transition to animal-free testing. Now entering its fifth decade, Convention ETS No. 123 continues to deliver on its promise to enhance animal well-being in science.

Hezbollah has not yet commented on the destruction. The conflict has intensified in Beirut, where strikes hit multiple locations, killing at least 12 people and wounding 27, according to Lebanese authorities. The Lebanese health ministry reports that 968 people, including at least 111 children, have been killed since the conflict escalated on March 2. Lebanon was drawn into the broader US-Israel war against Iran after Iran-backed Hezbollah fired missiles into Israel, prompting Israeli air strikes and the deployment of ground troops into southern Lebanon. The Litani River remains central to Lebanon's fragile security landscape. Under UN Security Council Resolution 1701, which ended the 2006 war, armed groups are barred from operating south of the river, though Israel asserts Hezbollah maintains a significant presence there. The Zrarieh Bridge was damaged in an earlier strike on Friday. The latest evacuation warnings have deepened a severe displacement crisis, with more than a million people forced from their homes, primarily in the south, east, and southern Beirut. Israel claims to be targeting not only fighters but also businesses financing military operations, including the Al Qard Al Hassan organisation.
