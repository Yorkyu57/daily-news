    [Original] Insulin Pump Recall: Insulet Removes Omnipod Pods
    The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has classified a recall of certain Omnipod insulin pumps by Insulet as Class I, the most serious type, indicating use may cause serious injury or death. The recall involves the removal of specific Omnipod Pods from use or sale, and the FDA’s recommendations remain unchanged. Affected devices are from specific lots. Users are instructed not to use Pods from these lots, check all Pods to determine if affected, and immediately change any Pod from an affected lot to resume insulin delivery. If users lack sufficient Pods due to the issue, they should consult their healthcare provider about alternative insulin delivery methods. On May 26, Insulet sent letters to affected customers. The company identified that some Pods from specific lots may have a small tear in the cannula (tubing) just above the skin, between the Pod and the point of entry. This can cause insulin to leak outside the Pod instead of being fully delivered, potentially leading to under-delivery. Users may notice wetness on the skin or Pod adhesive, or smell insulin, but in some cases, the leak may be difficult to detect. If insulin leakage occurs, blood glucose levels may rise and not respond as expected. For Omnipod 5 users in Automated Mode, this could trigger an Automated Delivery Restriction alert, but the issue may occur without any alert, meaning users could receive less insulin than needed unknowingly. Users are advised not to rely solely on Pod alerts. Prolonged high blood glucose due to under-delivery can lead to diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), a serious condition requiring prompt medical treatment that can be life-threatening. The issue does not affect continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems or readings. As of May 20, Insulet has reported 24 serious injuries and no deaths associated with this issue. Omnipod is intended for subcutaneous insulin delivery for diabetes management. U.S. customers with adverse reactions, quality problems, or questions should contact Insulet at https://www.omnipod.com/current-podders or call 1-800-641-2049. Healthcare professionals and consumers may also report adverse reactions or quality problems to the FDA’s MedWatch program.
