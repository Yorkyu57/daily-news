    [Original] Insulet Initiates Voluntary Medical Device Correction for Certain Omnipod® 5 Pods in the U.S.
    Insulet Corporation (NASDAQ: PODD) has initiated a voluntary Medical Device Correction for specific lots of Omnipod® 5 Pods distributed in the United States following the identification of a manufacturing issue. The company confirmed that this action applies only to identified lots, while all other Omnipod® 5 Pods and Omnipod® products remain safe to use. Insulet discovered that certain Pods from these specific lots may contain a small tear in the internal tubing responsible for insulin delivery. If this defect occurs, insulin may leak inside the Pod rather than being fully infused into the body as intended. Improper insulin delivery can result in high blood glucose levels due to under-delivery. In severe cases, prolonged and persistent high blood glucose levels may lead to diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), a serious medical condition requiring prompt treatment. Insulet has received 18 reports of serious adverse events associated with high blood glucose levels, including hospitalizations and DKA. No deaths have been reported. The company clarified that this issue does not affect continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems or CGM readings. Following its investigation, Insulet has implemented updates to its manufacturing processes and quality controls to strengthen detection and prevention, further supporting product integrity. The Pods involved in this correction represent approximately 1.5% of annual Omnipod® 5 pod production globally. Insulet continues to manufacture and ship Omnipod® 5 Pods and does not anticipate disruption to customer shipments, product availability, or new patient starts. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has been notified of this action, with patient safety and product quality remaining Insulet's highest priorities. Omnipod® 5 users are instructed to visit omnipod.com/check-pods to confirm whether their Pod lot number is included in this voluntary correction and to request replacement Pods at no cost. If a Pod from an affected lot is currently in use, customers should discontinue use immediately and replace it with a Pod from an unaffected lot. Customers requiring assistance may contact Insulet Product Support at 1-800-641-2049, available 24/7, or utilize the live agent chat at omnipod.com/current-podders. Headquartered in Massachusetts, Insulet Corporation is an innovative medical device company dedicated to simplifying life for people with diabetes through its Omnipod® product platform.
